Most recent: CodeWisp (YC W26)

At CodeWisp, an AI game-generation platform, I built the generation pipeline as a chain of agentic stages, working directly with the founder in twice-weekly product calls.

  • Sequential agent stages: routing, research, build, art, audio, quality gate and publish.
  • Multi-model routing across Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku, matched to task complexity and kept under a fixed $6 per-run cost cap.
  • An LLM-as-judge eval stage that checks sprite consistency, level passability, win states and mechanics, retries on failure and logs findings to improve the system prompts.
  • AI code generation constrained to a Lua sandbox to cut hallucinated code.
  • A deterministic 2D/3D runtime at 60 fps with hash-based verification and per-game engine versioning.
